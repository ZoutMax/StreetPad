Looking around

Map

On the map the crosshair stays in the centre and both sticks pan the map under it. A clicks at the crosshair, the triggers zoom there, and holding Y shows the blue Street View lines: release Y on a line to open Street View. The response curve and dead zone above apply too.

Walking & zoom

Street View moves in steps between photo spots; holding the stick forward keeps walking.

Buttons

Each button has a tap action and an optional hold action (hold ½ second). Buttons with a hold action fire their tap action on release. Walking, turning and run work while held, so they can't have a hold action.

Bookmarks

Save the current place with X; browse with D-pad ← / →. Click a name to rename it.

Google sign-in & privacy

The login mode changes after a restart.
Settings are stored in the registry (HKCU\Software\StreetPad), not in files.
